The teams map of type Map<String, Map<String, Integer>> consists of national football team names paired with a map of that team's goalscorers. The key of the inner map is the player's name, and the value is the number of goals scored by that player in the FIFA World Cup. An example dataset is provided in the DataLoader.loadTeams method and is shown at the end of this text.

Your task is to complete two methods in the Task class.

  1. Method
Stream<TeamInfo> successfulTeams(Map<String, Map<String, Integer>> teams, int minNoOfScorers, int minGoals)

must return a stream of national teams that have at least minNoOfScorers players who scored at least minGoals goals.

TeamInfo is defined as:

public record TeamInfo(String name, int noOfScorers) {}

For each national team that satisfies the condition, the TeamInfo object must contain:

- name: the name of the national team
- noOfScorers: the total number of goalscorers of that team

Example:

If minNoOfScorers is equal to 2 and minGoals is equal to 5, the method should return the national teams that have at least two players with at least five goals scored.

For the provided data, the result may contain:

TeamInfo[name=Brazil, noOfScorers=4]
TeamInfo[name=France, noOfScorers=3]
TeamInfo[name=Croatia, noOfScorers=5]

Important: noOfScorers is not the number of players satisfying the minGoals condition. It represents the total number of goalscorers for the national team.

  1. Method
Stream<TeamInfo> byScorersAndName(Stream<TeamInfo> teams)

must, without consuming the stream, modify it so that it returns the national teams sorted in descending order by the number of goalscorers. If the number of goalscorers is the same, the teams should be sorted by their name.

Example output for the byScorersAndName method:

TeamInfo[name=Argentina, noOfScorers=5]
TeamInfo[name=Croatia, noOfScorers=5]
TeamInfo[name=Brazil, noOfScorers=4]
TeamInfo[name=France, noOfScorers=3]
TeamInfo[name=Netherlands, noOfScorers=3]
TeamInfo[name=Canada, noOfScorers=1]
